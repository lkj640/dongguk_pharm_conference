Welcome

2026 marks a landmark year for Dongguk University — the 120th anniversary of its founding and the 15th anniversary of the College of Pharmacy. Since 2011, the College has steadily built its research and education foundation, earning an S-grade in the National Research Foundation of Korea's stage evaluation.

Today, nine of our faculty rank among the world's top 2% of researchers. Backed by 3,212 m² of research space, 500+ core instruments and 20 GPUs, the College has built a translational pipeline spanning discovery, design, delivery and evaluation of new drugs — and connects that work to the world through an MOU with the Luxembourg Institute of Health.

This symposium brings researchers from Korea and abroad together to share these achievements and explore the next generation of drug discovery.

— Kyeong Lee, Dean, Dongguk University College of Pharmacy

This symposium marks both the University's 120th founding anniversary and the College of Pharmacy's 15th anniversary — framing a shared theme of heritage meeting innovation in next-generation drug discovery.
